In-house tow dispatcher vs. outsourced dispatch: the true cost

A dispatcher's salary is the smallest part of the question. Here's what one full-time seat really costs a towing company, what it takes to cover all 168 hours of the week, and when outsourcing makes sense.

What one full-time dispatcher really costs

Start with a $50,000 salary for a full-time towing dispatcher and add what comes with the seat. These are the standard assumptions in our ROI calculator; change any of them to match your shop.

Cost of one full-time seatPer year
Base salary$50,000
Payroll taxes and benefits (35%)$17,500
Overtime to cover time off
Half of 256 missed hours, at 1.5× pay		$4,615
Turnover and retraining
40% leave each year × $6,000 to replace		$2,400
True cost of one seat$74,515

Even after paying that overtime, about 128 hours a year at that seat still go uncovered: the other half of vacation, holidays, sick days and empty weeks after someone quits.

What it takes to cover every hour

A week has 168 hours. A Monday-to-Friday, 8-to-4 dispatcher covers 40 of them. Covering the rest in-house usually takes:

  • day dispatchers sized to your fleet (roughly one per 15 trucks)
  • an evening dispatcher, Monday to Friday, 4pm to midnight
  • an overnight dispatcher, Monday to Friday, midnight to 8am
  • two weekend part-timers on 12-hour shifts

For a 30-truck fleet that's four full-time seats and two part-timers, about $364,000 a year. towvantage 24/7 Coverage for 30 trucks is $135,000 a year ($11,250 a month), about 63% less.

The costs that don't show up on payroll

  • Calls that ring out. When the desk is closed or overwhelmed, the caller tries the next company on the list.
  • Misquoted and misbilled tows. Rushed quotes and missed services lower revenue per tow. Our Texas client's revenue per call rose 55% once every call was quoted at full rate.
  • Drivers dispatching themselves. A driver answering the phone between jobs isn't driving.
  • Owners and managers covering the phones. Every no-show lands on someone who should be running the business.
  • Motor club and police rotation scores. Missed or slow responses can cost you calls later.

When in-house dispatch still makes sense

If you have a strong, stable day team you trust, you don't have to replace it. Nights & Weekends covers weekday overnights and weekends for $4,500 a month, with weekday evenings as an add-on. Dispatch

Will my customers talk to a real person?

Yes, on every call. A trained towvantage dispatcher answers live, day or night. Your customers aren't routed through phone trees or automated agents; they get a calm, knowledgeable person who books the job.

How is this different from a generic call center or answering service?

Our dispatchers are recruited and trained specifically on roadside terminology, motor club protocols and driver coordination before they ever touch a live call for your fleet. They dispatch and book jobs instead of taking messages.

How fast do you answer?

Our average answer time is under 45 seconds.

Do we need to replace our dispatch software?

No. We work inside the systems you already use, including TowBook, Beacon, Dispatch Anywhere and most major platforms.

Do you handle motor club and police rotation calls?

Yes. Motor club and police rotation calls are handled to protect your scores.

How fast can we go live?

Most clients are fully live within about two weeks of signing, including building your playbook, training and system setup.

How much does towvantage cost?

24/7 Coverage starts at $9,000 a month for up to 15 trucks. Nights & Weekends is $4,500 a month, and weekday evenings add $2,250. There's a one-time $1,500 setup fee, credited back in month 3. Build your quote or see pricing by fleet size.

How much can we save compared with in-house dispatch?

Clients typically pay about half of what their in-house dispatch cost. Our Illinois client went from $576,000 a year to $264,000. Use the ROI calculator with your own numbers.

Can we keep our day dispatchers?

Yes. Nights & Weekends covers weekday overnights and all weekend while your team keeps days. Add weekday evenings if your day team goes home at 4pm.

What if it doesn't work out?

Every plan is backed by a 30-day performance guarantee. If a dispatcher isn't meeting your standard, we retrain or replace them at no cost. If it's still not right for you, you walk. We're month-to-month, always.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. towvantage is month-to-month, with no long-term contract, ever. We have to re-earn your business every month, which is how we like it.

What else can towvantage handle?

Back-office specialists for routing, AR and collections, safety management, heavy-duty sales and a contract towing desk. Each is $2,500 a month full time or $1,250 half time, dedicated to your fleet.
